IWMAC Vector

An unmodified copy of the official web build of VectorCraft 0.9.0, an open-source vector editor, hosted for the IWMAC Designer Import/Export userscript. Its Edit in VectorCraft choice opens a panel's background here and puts what you save back on the panel.

Open the editor

Everything runs in your browser: nothing you open or draw here is sent anywhere.
