IWMAC Vector

VectorCraft 0.14.0, an open-source vector editor, built from its official source for the IWMAC Designer Import/Export userscript, with WebAssembly SIMD turned on: it draws about twice as fast as the official web build. Its Edit in VectorCraft choice opens a panel's background here and puts what you save back on the panel.

Open the editor

Everything runs in your browser: nothing you open or draw here is sent anywhere.

Based on VectorCraft by the ArtCraft team, licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0 (MIT, Apache-2.0; fonts under the SIL Open Font License). This build differs from the official one: it is built with WebAssembly SIMD, it takes a fix its release does not have yet (see the README), and it carries no ArtCraft name, wordmark or mark. It is not made, endorsed or supported by the ArtCraft team. Source: github.com/storytold/vectorcraft; how this build is made: github.com/Hapnes-dev/iwmac-vector.
